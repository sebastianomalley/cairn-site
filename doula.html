This might be for you if...

Nothing here asks you to commit to anything. Take it one step at a time.

  • You or someone you love has received a hard diagnosis
  • You're caring for a parent, partner or friend and feeling stretched thin
  • Hospice is involved, and you'd like more time and support around it
  • You want your wishes written down while there's no rush
  • You're not sure what to expect at the end and want someone steady beside you
  • Your family has its own faith, culture or traditions you want honored
  • You've lost someone and don't know where to start
  • Saying goodbye to an animal companion? I'm trained in that too. Here's how I help

For everyone

Every faith, every family, every way of saying goodbye.

There's no one right way to die, grieve or remember. Your beliefs, your culture and your family's traditions lead. I follow.

I'll take the time to learn what matters to you, and help bring in the people and practices that make it feel like yours, from your faith leader to your grandmother's prayers to the music you grew up on.

"I'm not here to fix anything. I'm here to show up, and to make sure you don't have to do this alone."

Jason O'Malley

Non-medical end-of-life support in Washington, DC, Northern Virginia, Florida and North Carolina, with virtual support anywhere. Every faith, culture and family welcome.
