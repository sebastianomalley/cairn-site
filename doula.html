Welcome

However you got here, you're welcome.

Maybe you're caring for someone you love. Maybe you're planning ahead. Maybe someone mentioned a "death doula" and you're quietly wondering what that even means.

This can feel like a lot. That's normal. This site is set up to walk you through it one step at a time, at whatever pace feels okay. Nothing here asks you to commit to anything.

If at any point you'd rather just talk, I'm a phone call away.

For everyone

Every faith, every family, every way of saying goodbye.

There's no one right way to die, grieve or remember. Your beliefs, your culture and your family's traditions lead. I follow.

I'll take the time to learn what matters to you, and help bring in the people and practices that make it feel like yours, from your faith leader to your grandmother's prayers to the music you grew up on.
