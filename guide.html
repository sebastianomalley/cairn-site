End-of-life doula care

For people, and for the animals who share our lives.

Dying is one of the few things everyone goes through, and almost nobody is shown what to expect. I sit with people and families before, during and after, so nobody has to figure it out alone.

Book a consult

What a doula does

A doula is non-medical support. I don't replace your doctor, vet or hospice team. I'm the steady person in the room who has time to listen, explain what's happening, and help you make choices you can live with.

  • Being present, at the bedside, at home, or at the vet
  • Making a plan: your wishes, the hard conversations, who needs to know what
  • Legacy projects like letters, recordings and life stories
  • Support for the family and the people who are caregiving
  • Vigil support in the last days
  • Being around after the loss, when everyone else has gone back to normal

Two kinds of care

For people

Whether you're facing your own ending or walking a parent, partner or friend toward theirs, I help you get clear on what matters, say what needs saying, and be present for the time that's left.

For companion animals

For a lot of us, an animal is family, and the loss is real. I help you understand what your animal is going through, weigh the hard decisions, and make their last days gentle for both of you.
