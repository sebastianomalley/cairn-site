Back Nine

Ten weeks. Real talk. Brothers who show up.

What happens in the room

We meet once a week for ten weeks. Each week has a theme, a short reading or video to sit with beforehand (we call it game film), and a circle where we talk about it. Some nights start with a poem. Some start with breath work. Nobody has to perform.

I facilitate. I'm not there to fix anyone or hand out advice. The point is to be seen and heard by other men, to say the true thing and have it land, and to let whatever comes up come up.

We talk about love, death, family, purpose, brotherhood. Spirituality is on the table, but it's yours to figure out. Nobody's going to push a belief on you, and you don't need one to be here.
