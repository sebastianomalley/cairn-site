Privacy

Your story stays yours

i.

Confidential, always

What you share with me stays with me. Confidentiality is written into our agreement.

ii.

Nothing shared without your okay

Stories, photos and films are never shared or posted without your written permission.

iii.

Only what's needed

I keep only the notes I need to support you well, and I keep them secure.

iv.

Not a medical provider

I don't provide medical care or keep medical records. Your care team handles that.

Practical questions

The logistics

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you'd like support with, so we'll talk it through on the free call. Everything is agreed in writing before we begin, with no surprises.

Is it covered by insurance?

Not yet. Doula care is paid privately today, though many of us are working to change that.

Where do you work?

In person in Washington, DC, Northern Virginia, Florida and North Carolina, and virtually anywhere.

What if I'm not ready for all this?

That's completely okay. You can read, sit with it, and come back when you're ready. Or start on your own with the workbook.

Can I just talk once?

Of course. One conversation is sometimes exactly what someone needs.
