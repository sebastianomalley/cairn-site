Recommended reading

Books I trust

Some go-to reads for families, caregivers and anyone curious. Take what helps.

For anyone facing the end, and the people beside them

  • Final GiftsMaggie Callanan and Patricia KelleyTwo hospice nurses on understanding what the dying may be trying to tell us.
  • Being MortalAtul GawandeA surgeon on what matters most at the end of life, and how medicine can honor it.
  • A Beginner's Guide to the EndBJ Miller and Shoshana BergerWarm, practical and surprisingly funny. The paperwork, the care, the feelings.
  • The Art of Dying WellKaty ButlerA clear guide to planning for a good end, stage by stage.
  • Gone from My SightBarbara KarnesThe small booklet many hospices hand families about the final weeks.

For grief

  • It's OK That You're Not OKMegan DevineGrief without the pressure to get over it.
  • The Wild Edge of SorrowFrancis WellerOn grief as something we carry together.

For children

  • The Invisible StringPatrice KarstA gentle picture book about staying connected to the people we love.
  • LifetimesBryan Mellonie and Robert IngpenA simple way to talk with kids about beginnings and endings.

For pets, and for a lighter touch

  • Goodbye, FriendGary KowalskiFor anyone who has loved and lost an animal.
  • Smoke Gets in Your EyesCaitlin DoughtyA funny, honest look at death from a funeral director.

Links go to Bookshop.org, which supports independent bookstores.

Official forms

Make it official

An advance directive lets you name who speaks for you and write down your medical wishes. Every state has its own version, so always check the official source.

CaringInfo

Free advance directive forms for every state. Helpline 800-658-8898.

https://www.caringinfo.org/planning/advance-directives/by-state/
The Conversation Project

Free guides for talking with family about what matters.

https://theconversationproject.org
DC MOST form

Medical orders for people who are seriously ill in DC. Ask your doctor.

https://dchealth.dc.gov/most
Virginia Advance Directive Registry

Store yours so providers can find it.

https://www.connectvirginia.org/adr/
Florida advance directives

Living will, health care surrogate and donation forms.

https://quality.healthfinder.fl.gov/report-guides/advance-directives
North Carolina Advance Health Care Directive Registry

Documents must be notarized to register.

https://www.sosnc.gov/ahc
These links go to outside organizations. I'm not a lawyer or a medical provider, so when in doubt, ask your doctor, hospice team or an attorney.
