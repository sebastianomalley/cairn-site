Make it official

An advance directive lets you name who speaks for you and write down your medical wishes. Every state has its own version.

CaringInfo

Free advance directive forms and instructions for every state. Helpline 800-658-8898.

https://www.caringinfo.org/planning/advance-directives/by-state/

The Conversation Project

Free guides for talking with family about what matters.

https://theconversationproject.org

PREPARE for Your Care

Easy-to-read advance directives and short videos.

https://prepareforyourcare.org

Organ and tissue donation

Sign up to be a donor.
