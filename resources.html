Official forms

Make it official

An advance directive lets you name who speaks for you and write down your medical wishes. Every state has its own version, so always check the official source.

CaringInfo

Free advance directive forms for every state. Helpline 800-658-8898.

https://www.caringinfo.org/planning/advance-directives/by-state/
The Conversation Project

Free guides for talking with family about what matters.

https://theconversationproject.org
DC MOST form

Medical orders for people who are seriously ill in DC. Ask your doctor.

https://dchealth.dc.gov/most
Virginia Advance Directive Registry

Store yours so providers can find it.

https://www.connectvirginia.org/adr/
Florida advance directives

Living will, health care surrogate and donation forms.

https://quality.healthfinder.fl.gov/report-guides/advance-directives
North Carolina Advance Health Care Directive Registry

Documents must be notarized to register.

https://www.sosnc.gov/ahc
These links go to outside organizations. I'm not a lawyer or a medical provider, so when in doubt, ask your doctor, hospice team or an attorney.
