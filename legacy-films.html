Legacy films

Your story, in your own voice.

A short film of a life, made for the people who will want to hear you tell it again.

Why I do this

Before this work, I spent years behind a camera.

Photos hold a face. A film holds the way someone laughs, the pause before the punchline, the way they say your name.

A legacy film is a short, personal portrait. We sit down together, I ask good questions, and you tell it your way. The funny parts, the hard parts and the stuff nobody's heard before. It's yours to keep and share however you like, or not at all.

How it goes

Simple, unhurried, yours.

We talk first

What do you want to say, and who is it for? We choose the questions and the place together.

We film

At home or somewhere that matters to you. One relaxed session, with breaks whenever you need them.

You keep it

I edit it into a short film you can watch, share and pass down. Nothing is shared without your okay.

"Tell me about a time you laughed so hard you couldn't stop."

One of the questions I like to start with.

Legacy films can be part of our work together or a project on their own. Every film is different, so we'll talk through it on a free call.
