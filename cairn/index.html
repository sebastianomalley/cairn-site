The circle

A circle, not a class.

Cairn is a small group of like-minded men who meet regularly to talk about the things that matter. No judgment, no pressure, no one trying to fix anyone.

There's a loose path we walk together, but nothing is asked of you except showing up. Share where you're at, or just sit and listen. Both are welcome, every time.

Come as you are

All you have to do is show up.

Listen

Some nights you'll just want to take it in. That's enough.

Share

When you're ready, say what's true for you, wherever you're at.

Belong

The same faces, week after week, and a room where you don't have to perform.

Purpose. Family. Brotherhood. Death and life. Love. And whatever you walk in with.

Why Cairn

Each man adds a stone.

A cairn is a stack of stones built by people who walked a path before you, left to mark the way for whoever comes next. Nobody builds one alone. Each person who passes adds a stone.

That's the idea here. The men in each circle share what they've learned the hard way, and leave the path a little clearer for the men who come after them.

About

Why I started Cairn

Being part of a men's group changed something in me. Sitting in a room with like-minded men, with no judgment and no pressure, I found I could just be where I was. Some weeks I shared. Some weeks I listened. Both were enough.

It changed how I show up, for myself and for the people in my life. Cairn is my way of making that kind of space for other men.

I'm not here to lead anyone anywhere. I'm here to guide, hold the space, and add my stone alongside yours.

Jason O'Malley
