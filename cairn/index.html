The circle

A circle, not a class.

Most men don't have many places where they can say what's really going on. Not the highlight reel, the real stuff. Cairn is that place.

A small group of men meets once a week for ten weeks. Each week has a theme, something to read or watch, and a lot of honest conversation. No one is there to fix you, and no one has to perform. You show up, you listen, and you say what's true for you.

I'm not a guru and I'm not here to lead you anywhere. I'm a guide. My job is to hold the space so the group can do the work.

Ten weeks

One theme each week

  1. i.Purpose
  2. ii.Death and life
  3. iii.Soul
  4. iv.Family
  5. v.Spirituality
  6. vi.Health
  7. vii.Brotherhood
  8. viii.Adventure
  9. ix.Love
  10. x.Self

Any faith or none. Any background. Every man welcome.

Why Cairn

Each man adds a stone.

A cairn is a stack of stones built by people who walked a path before you, left to mark the way for whoever comes next. Nobody builds one alone. Each person who passes adds a stone.

That's the idea here. The men in each circle share what they've learned the hard way, and leave the path a little clearer for the men who come after them.

How it works

Simple by design

Small

A capped group, so everyone gets heard and the same faces show up each week.

Weekly

Same time every week for ten weeks, in the DC, Maryland and Virginia area.

Ongoing

New circles start on a rotation. Some men come back to guide the next one.
