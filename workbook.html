The workbook

Leaving Well

Not ready for a call? That's completely okay. Start here, privately, whenever you're ready.

Leaving Well is a guided workbook that walks you through many of the same questions I explore with the people I support. It's warm, a little fun, and nothing like a form at the doctor's office. For some people it's all they need. For others it's a comfortable first step.
