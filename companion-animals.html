Companion animal care

For the animals we love.

They're family. Saying goodbye to them deserves the same care, time and gentleness as any other goodbye.

Why it matters

Real love, real grief

Losing an animal can hit as hard as losing a person. Sometimes harder, because the world doesn't always make room for it.

I trained in companion animal end-of-life care because I know that bond. My own dogs are a big part of my life. I'm here to help you make the hard decisions with less fear, plan a goodbye that feels right, and not feel alone in the grief that follows.

I don't provide veterinary care. I work alongside your vet, never in place of them.

How I can help

Before, during and after

Before

Thinking it through

Unhurried time to talk about quality of life, what your vet has shared, and what you're seeing at home, so you can decide when you're ready.

Before

Planning the goodbye

At home or at the clinic, who'll be there, a favorite blanket, a last walk, a special meal. The small things that make it theirs.

During

Being there

Steady company for you and your family through the final hours, whether it's a planned euthanasia or a natural passing at home.

During

Helping the kids

Gentle, honest ways to talk with children about what's happening, and ways for them to say goodbye too.

After

Aftercare and remembering

Talking through burial or cremation, paw prints, keepsakes and small rituals to honor them.

After

Grief support

Checking in during the weeks after, and making room for a grief that's often brushed aside.

Getting started

It starts with a free call

Twenty minutes to tell me about your animal, what's going on, and what you're hoping for.

From there we decide together what support looks like. Sometimes it's one visit. Sometimes it's a few weeks of walking alongside you. You get a simple written agreement with the cost up front.
