You design it

Built around you

Some people want one good conversation. Some want me there every step of the way. Most land somewhere in between.

Whatever you choose, it's all about companioning you and the people you love, in a way that fits your life, your beliefs and your family. We'll shape it together on a free call, and it can change as things change.

Part of vigil and legacy support

Legacy films

Before this work I spent years behind a camera. A legacy film is a short, personal portrait of a life told in your own voice, for the people who'll want to hear it again. We talk first, film somewhere that feels right, and nothing is shared without your okay.

Your traditions lead

Your way, always

Every culture has its own wisdom about death, and every family has its own way of saying goodbye.

Whether that means prayers in a certain language, a faith leader at the bedside, specific rituals after a death, or no religion at all, I'll learn what matters to you and help make room for it. If I don't know something, I'll ask, and I'll listen.
